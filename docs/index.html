FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about SatConnect.
What is an eSIM?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM that allows you to activate a cellular plan without a physical SIM card. It is built into most modern smartphones (iPhone XS and newer).

Is my phone compatible?

SatConnect works with any eSIM-compatible device running iOS 16.0 or later. This includes iPhone XS, XR, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and all Pro/Max variants.

Can I keep my regular phone number?

Yes! Your eSIM works alongside your regular SIM (Dual SIM). Keep your number for calls/texts and use SatConnect for data while traveling.

How fast is the data?

SatConnect uses local carrier networks in each country, providing 4G/LTE speeds. Typical speeds range from 20-100 Mbps depending on location and network conditions.

What happens when my data runs out?

You will receive a notification when running low. You can purchase additional data directly in the app at any time - activation is instant.

Is there a refund policy?

If your eSIM has not been activated, you are eligible for a full refund. Once activated, refunds are handled case-by-case. Contact support for assistance.

Do I need Wi-Fi to activate?

Yes, you need an internet connection (Wi-Fi or existing cellular) to download and activate your eSIM. After that, you are good to go!
