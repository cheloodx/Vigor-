FAQ
Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about SatConnect. Can not find the answer? Contact support.

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM that allows you to activate a cellular plan without a physical SIM card. It is built into most modern smartphones including iPhone XS and newer.

Is my phone compatible?

SatConnect works with any eSIM-compatible device running iOS 16.0 or later. This includes iPhone XS, XR, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and all Pro/Max variants.

Can I keep my regular phone number?

Yes! Your eSIM works alongside your regular SIM (Dual SIM). Keep your number for calls and texts while using SatConnect for affordable data abroad.

How fast is the data?

SatConnect uses local carrier networks providing 4G/LTE and 5G speeds. Typical speeds range from 20-100 Mbps depending on your location.

What happens when my data runs out?

You will receive a notification when running low. Purchase additional data directly in the app at any time - activation is instant.

Is there a refund policy?

If your eSIM has not been activated, you are eligible for a full refund. Once activated, refunds are handled case-by-case. Contact our support team.

Do I need Wi-Fi to activate?

Yes, you need an internet connection (Wi-Fi or existing cellular data) to download and activate your eSIM. After activation, you are good to go!
