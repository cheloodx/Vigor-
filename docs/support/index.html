Frequently Asked Questions

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM that allows you to activate a cellular plan without using a physical SIM card. Most modern smartphones support eSIM technology.

How do I activate my eSIM?

After purchasing a plan in the SatConnect app, you'll receive an activation QR code. Go to your phone's Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM and scan the QR code. Your data plan will be activated within minutes.

Which countries are supported?

SatConnect offers eSIM plans for 50+ countries worldwide, including popular destinations across Europe, Asia, Americas, and more. Check the app for the full list of available countries and plans.

Is my device compatible?

SatConnect requires an eSIM-compatible device running iOS 16.0 or later. Most iPhone models from iPhone XS (2018) and newer support eSIM.

How are payments processed?

All payments are securely processed through Stripe. We never store or have access to your credit card details. You can pay with credit card, debit card, or Apple Pay.

Can I get a refund?

If your eSIM has not been activated, you may be eligible for a full refund. Once activated, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact our support team for assistance.

My eSIM isn't working. What should I do?

Try these steps: 1) Ensure eSIM is enabled in Settings > Cellular. 2) Toggle Airplane Mode on and off. 3) Restart your device. 4) Check that Data Roaming is enabled. If the issue persists, contact our support team.

Can I use my existing SIM and eSIM at the same time?

Yes! Most eSIM-compatible devices support Dual SIM, allowing you to use your regular SIM for calls/texts and the SatConnect eSIM for data while traveling.
